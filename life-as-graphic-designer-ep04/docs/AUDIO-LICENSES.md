# EP04 media provenance

- `public/audio/ep04-voiceover.mp3`: user-supplied final nasal ElevenLabs recording, original bytes preserved. No new spoken dialogue, nasal EQ, pitch/time processing or duplicated sniffles/breaths.
- `public/audio/music-original.wav`: original deterministic 80 BPM lo-fi composition generated offline by `scripts/prepare-episode-audio.py`. No commercial song or sampled recording.
- `send` and `desk-ambience` WAVs: original sine synthesis in that script. Quiet desk tone contains no human breath or sniffle.
- Remaining series clicks/pops/typing/notification/impact/success/romance-slip/ending effects: original procedural DSP assets, with generation source preserved in the adjacent EP01–EP03 projects in this repository. No external commercial samples.
- Original Space Grotesk font: SIL Open Font License, preserved next to the bundled WOFF2.
- Great Vibes, Archivo Black, Libre Baskerville: SIL OFL fonts bundled locally from pinned Fontsource packages; their licenses are retained in `public/fonts/`. They are used only to demonstrate mismatched typography inside the fictional poster. Editorial text remains Space Grotesk.

The character, tissue prop, workstation and poster are SVG/CSS artwork. No stock footage, newly generated character images or unrelated 3D scenes are used.
